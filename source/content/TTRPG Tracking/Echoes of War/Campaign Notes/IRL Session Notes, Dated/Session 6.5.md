"[[Ysgraviir]]'s Lost Day"

Took place 8-29-26, in-game Tarsakh 2nd 1487

__BEGIN TARSAKH 2__

[[Ysgraviir]], seeking to avoid the dangerous [[Callie O'Toole]], gets up early and books it out of the [[Red Herring Inn & Tavern]], seeking to enjoy the festivities of the day in peace.  He is approached by a down-on-his-luck-looking fellow, who voices a need for his services; namely, the fact that it seems [[Ysgraviir]] can make good, violent use of his axe. [[Ysgraviir]]complies with the offer and follows the stranger. They go through the city, into the seedier parts, eventually to an underground area, terminating in a dungeon-esque room smelling of dung, musk, and . . . reptiles? The walls are lined with cages, filled with various monstrosities. The stranger drops his hood, revealing a hideously scarred face, and introduces himself as "The Monster Keeper". He offers [[Ysgraviir]] honor, glory, and coin in return for testing his monsters, and the offer is once again accepted. Our favorite "just a guy" proceeds to mow down roughly half of The Monster Keeper's stock, before tapping out in a bout with a large shark-like creature; The Keeper calls it back to its cage and thanks [[Ysgraviir]] for revealing the strongest of his monsters, and offers 20 lions in payment. The Monster Keeper then beseeches [[Ysgraviir]] to keep this outing a secret, promising he'll be made a champion if he returns tomorrow. [[Ysgraviir]] then departs, seeking food and drink NOT offered by a horned baddie; this leads him to The Dancing Feet, a tavern with two entrances: a large and small foot-shaped door. [[Ysgraviir]] proceeds through the large door, and is met with a curious scene: A large, open floor plan tavern, with a wraparound style bar in the center, which slopes downward as it circles, with chairs matching the height all the way. The tables and chairs are organized similarly throughout the place, as all altitudinal persuasions are catered to; also, as the name of the establishment would imply, there are several open areas where people are free to dance. [[Ysgraviir]] seats himself near the "comically large" section of the tavern, pleased to finally sit in a seat worthy of his physical stature; just as he settles in, a large cup of Suzale is slid to him by a gnome, who quickly scurries off as [[Ysgraviir]] nods in appreciation. Soon after, a human waitress approaches, offering from the tavern's endless stew; [[Ysgraviir]] accepts. Eating and drinking, he notes that no pricing has been mentioned yet, and decides to refrain from ordering any more sustenance until he can figure out what the pricing scale is; despite this, he is brought more ale and stew, which he consumes. When offered a third round, [[Ysgraviir]] refuses, asking what his bill is looking like at this point; the waitress is quick to point out that the food and drink has been paid for, courtesy of the one and only Ted Herren (first appearance in [[Session 3]]). The waitress is a bit starstruck that [[Ysgraviir]] seems to be connected to "The Second Greatest Bard in Cormyr" and seeks to know what makes him so special; [[Ysgraviir]] stays humble and stumbles through proving he knows that guy beyond his name. [[Ysgraviir]], taking his well-sized chair with him, goes to Ted and thanks him for the comped meal, apologizing for his smart comments from their last encounter. Ted takes it in stride, lambasting [[Ysgraviir]] for not knowing his famous saying: "There's too many monsters to go making foes, so bury your hatchets and drown all your woes". Noticing a tinge of sadness in the bard's voice, [[Ysgraviir]] asks what's up; after an odd back and forth, Ted tells of his past as an orphan among orphans, back in his fighter days. He formed an adventuring crew that also played songs to uplift their fellow parentless; his whole group was killed by a Lich, with Ted alone escaping after firing a crossbow bolt into its undead nuts. Ted raises a toast to his departed friends, which [[Ysgraviir]] joins. Ted says he knew his adventuring days were over when "I found out that Elminster just went and killed that Lich", revealing to him that some folks are just built different, whilst he is not. Ted then takes the stage and plays a song that tells of that fateful day of his adventuring party's demise; it heals up [[Ysgraviir]], but kills the mood in the tavern. Ted, before departing, advises [[Ysgraviir]] to express his love to whoever he's holding it back from. [[Ysgraviir]] then rents a succubus-free room, bathes, and hits the hay.

__END OF TARSAKH 2__
