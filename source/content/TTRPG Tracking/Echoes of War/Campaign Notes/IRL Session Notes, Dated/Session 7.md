"Favored and Foes"

Took place 8-29-26, in-game Tarsakh 3rd 1487

*Our adventurers continue to enjoy the many wonders of the Festival of Heroes, feeling favored by royalty, the populace, and perhaps even the Gods. But acquiring the attention to be recognized comes at a cost: the eyes of allies and foes alike cannot be turned away . . .*

__THE NIGHT LEADING IN TO TARSAKH 3__

- [[Rook]] wakes up feeling uneasy. Deciding nothing is the matter, surely just a bad dream, he elects to go grab a glass of water before going back to sleep. As he gets to the common area, he notices a figure sitting on the couch, back turned to [[Rook]]. Sneaking a bit closer, the fellow is noted to be downright gross: patchy beard, horrid stench, gnarled hands, grown out nails, glassy eyes, the works; despite all that, he appears to be finely dressed. [[Rook]] heads back to bed, to fill [[Ember]] in on their visitor, letting her know he'll "take care of it". [[Ember]] voices a desire to talk to the guy while [[Rook]] lurks in the shadows in case things go south; they agree on that plan. [[Rook]] does [[Rook]] things and disappears into thin air, and [[Ember]] walks into the common area; upon noticing her, the man gets up and darts to a nearby window, hiding behind a curtain, leaving his nasty unshod feet poking out. The man left a grossly visible stain on the couch. [[Rook]] readies his bow. The stranger coughs. [[Ember]] makes it clear the man's efforts to hide himself are in vain, demanding he identify himself. He sucks his gut in a bit and remains silent. [[Ember]] sends a Message advising him to come out. He expresses his fear that folk will find him ugly, and that he wishes to see his friend [[Grandsire Na’thaniél]]. [[Ember]] and [[Rook]] go to fetch him, waking [[Ascánthir]] as well. [[Grandsire Na’thaniél]] comes out and announces himself to the stranger, who begins speaking as though they're great friends; [[Grandsire Na’thaniél]] clearly doesn't know the guy. The stranger jogs his memory with a short retelling of a time they shared together, and the two go to a corner to have a badly whispered discussion. [[Grandsire Na’thaniél]] adresses the man as The Watcher, and war is discussed: "It will be harder to keep the secret of Cormanthor, as the armies are moving." The Watcher explains that he has made himself appear disgusting on purpose so that he can sneak around unnoticed, and voices an urgent call for [[Grandsire Na’thaniél]] to return to the Council; he's pretty noncommital, but agrees enough to get the smelly fellow out of the Penthouse. [[Rook]] asks what all that was about. [[Ascánthir]] nods approval for [[Grandsire Na’thaniél]] to spill the beans, and he explains that an evil undead army is expanding towards the elven homeland of Cormanthor, but he isn't all that concerned, considering they'll have to get through the Dale Lands first; this perturbs [[Rook]], as his family is there. [[Rook]] asks how he can help, but is shut down: this is the job of armies, not an adventuring party.

- [[Tharrick]] seeks magic companents. He tracks down a wizardly looking fellow named Bromus, who demands not to be called The Brofessor. Bromus and his assistant lead [[Tharrick]] to a component shop just a stone's throw away. A cozy hut-like establishment with crystals and incense burning in the window, [[Tharrick]] enters to see a half-elven woman grinding some ingredients in a mortar and pestle. Introductions are exchanged, and [[Tharrick]] requests up to 600 lions worth of diamonds; the shopkeep is hesitant, as thieves target that stuff, and requests to see proof of payment. [[Tharrick]] presents his ample money pouch, and the shopkeep fetches the display case, calling an appraiser to make sure appropriate gems are selected. [[Tharrick]] offers 589 lions for 600 lions worth of gems, which the shopkeep accepts; despite this, he gives her his entire purse. [[Tharrick]] makes brief conversation about the aforementioned thieving problem, but lets the subject go. The shopkeep lets him know that return customers get a 1% discount, and he departs to the post office to meet with The Party.

- [[Ember]] and [[Rook]] break off from the group to hit up the Temple to Mystra, [[Tharrick]] in tow. It's still an active crime scene, bursting at the seams with Purple Dragon Knights, who instantly recognize and approach [[Rook]] and [[Ember]]. [[Ember]] lies about not knowing anything about the explosion, and asks to help investigate; the knights say eff it and let the trio past the security tape. The Temple interior looks. . . . melty? [[Rook]] sees evidence that the Temple was broken into after-hours, and signs of a significant group struggle pre-melt. [[Ember]] discovers the melted nature of the place was due to a massive sculpted Fireball, and resistance was met via Radiant spellcasting. After noting 5 unscathed spots in the room, the trio conclude that the perpetrators Teleported away, and they notice that a large marble pulpit seems to have been moved before the blast. [[Rook]] does [[Rook]] things and casually moves the massive thing aside, revealing a ladder leading down to a tunnel with a single pair of feminine (or pirate) footprints leading the way. The trail ends in a not very trafficked area near the docks of [[Suzail]]. [[Ember]] surmises this was where the Priestess met with the Red Hand for smuggling purposes; maybe she's still alive after all? [[Ember]] fills the guards in on the investigation, minus the Right Hand stuff, and the trio leaves.

*THE BOYS FOLLOW*

- [[Tharrick]], [[Ember]], and [[Rook]] head to the Thieve's Guild. They come back to the hidden room with the big table populated by guild members; a fellow appearing to be the guild master is at the head of the table, flanked by other folks, all looking at a map. Upon approach by the trio, people begin to draw weapons; a raised hand by the guild master calms things down. He gestures for [[Rook]] to approach. [[Rook]] asks for info on the smuggling tunnel under the Temple to Mystra. The guild master indicates they don't use that tunnel since The Hand seized control of it; in fact, The Hand has gotten control of a lot of the City's smuggling tunnels. Most of the more profitable ones were left to the Thieve's Guild, so tensions have been chill for the most part; however, 4 members have been murdered lately, and enough is enough. [[Rook]] points out a tunnel leading to the Royal Castle marked in both black (Thieve's) and red (Right Hand) and asks what the deal is. [[Tharrick]] guesses correctly: it's contested. [[Rook]] inquires where the Castle tunnel leads out to, and the guild master is hesitant to reveal such information. [[Tharrick]] presents a tattoo indicating his allegiance to his Thieve's Guild back home, and the trio is informed the tunnel leads out to the Royal Botanical Garden. The guild master informs [[Rook]] of the password needed to use it, charging the trio with investigating. [[Tharrick]] requests diamond dust to trap the exit against The Hand; his request is met. Short mention is made about a certain book the Right Hand are after, and the trio departs. [[Tharrick]] asks about the book; [[Ember]] says [[Ascánthir]] knows more about it than she does. They go to find THE BOYS.

After almost hitting a small child, a farmer looking fellow declares to any who will listen that [[Vaelithra]] is one of the Hilp 7 that saved the town, and starts a cheer. Uncomfortable with the unsolicited adoration, [[Vaelithra]] makes like a tree and leaves for the Gardens, arriving shortly thereafter. After a short disagreement with some Purple Dragon Knights about if [[Verdix]] is a steed or not, the drake and [[Vaelithra]] go into the Royal Botanical Gardens, where they come across [[Prince Baerovus Obarskyr]] and [[Lady Sarya Crownsilver]] sitting on a bench, and the Prince is oggling some druid lady in the distance. As [[Vaelithra]] approaches, he notices her; she bows, eliciting a smirk. [[Vaelithra]] boldly accuses [[Prince Baerovus Obarskyr]] of his wandering eyes, to which he takes mild offense. She then asks why he abdicated the throne, to which he takes stronger offense. He admits he believed [[Queen Raedra Obarskyr]] would be the stronger leader, and he prefers being an advisor anyways; you don't take the fall for failures that way. [[Vaelithra]], dissatisfied that the Prince has gone a full 5 seconds without being made uncomfortable, asks why his hair is white. He reveals it's due to a very severe trauma that has rendered him undable to feel anything anymore. [[Vaelithra]] says she can relate, as she has past trauma paired with a hair color change too, though not as extreme or related. [[Vaelithra]] then walks away to a secluded area to rest with [[Verdix]]. Some time passes, and she awakens to a shadow being cast over her by none other than [[Tinuvian]]! They chat about the festival a bit, and he declares his excitment for the fireworks, and makes it clear he's horny af. He also mentions being bff's with [[Ascánthir]], which prompts a short conversation about his and [[Grandsire Na’thaniél]]'s family dynamic. [[Vaelithra]] casts judgement on how horribly down bad [[Tinuvian]] is; he talks trash back about how Rangers suck. The two flirt for a bit, culmination with [[Tinuvian]] desiring to "train cardio" outside the city with her; she sort of but not entirely declines. [[Tinuvian]] laments his horniness, knowing he needs to settle down. [[Vaelithra]] recommends he pursue [[Zabrina Carpenterbee]], informing him she's hot stuff. [[Tinuvian]] departs, and [[Vaelithra]] finishes her rest, setting off to find THE BOYS.

- The Party finds THE BOYS prepping to stalk [[Irelyn]]. [[Rook]] spots a peculiar beggar, and drops a coin into his cup. The fellow uses Thieve's Cant to indicate a meeting is happening now. [[Rook]] asks where, and the beggar points to [[Irelyn]]. Petram the Purple Dragon Knight approaches, and he and [[Forge]] bro out for a bit. [[Ascánthir]] and [[Ven]] are wicked smaht, and know where Lord Brace Scatterhawk's estate is, and The Party sets off with [[Rook]] spearheading the sneaky stalking efforts. In the midst of this, [[Ascánthir]] and [[Tharrick]] discuss the Book of Vile Darkness; [[Ascánthir]] meantions Orcus, demon lord of the dead. [[Ascánthir]] retreats to an alleyway to let [[Mistress Ossani Eveningspire]] know what's up, and to teleport in; she obliges, dressed to sneak. They bicker for a bit, then rejoin the group. [[Rook]] close behind, [[Irelyn]] is let past some guards into the Scatterhawk Estate; seeking a point of ingress, [[Rook]] notices the estate shares a side gate entrance with a neighboring estate, secured by a sleeping guard. [[Rook]] does [[Rook]] things, and grabs the keys and waltzes right into the estate, and into Scatterhawk's. [[Vaelithra]] elects to stand watch on a nearby rooftop while the rest of the Party follow [[Rook]]'s route to spy on the meeting between [[Irelyn]] and Lord Brace Scatterhawk. Brace asks her if she's enjoying the festival; [[Irelyn]] declines the small talk and places several mithril shard onto his desk. Brace begins naming people as he slides each shard across the desk, indicating their association with armor she's made from those shards. Scatterhawk scolds her for keeping a shard from her; she presents [[Forge]]'s shard, but declines to name him. Brace pressures her, threatening her, until she finally gives his name; Brace claims to know him. [[Irelyn]] voices her desire to leave, but Brace shuts it down, offering her marriage to allow her to stay at the estate; she declines, saying she just wants to pay off her dad's debt. Brace indicates he just needs a shard for [[Queen Raedra Obarskyr]], and dismisses her, after offering something to her that she refuses. The Party clocks him being served blood to drink afterwards. [[Irelyn]] retreats to an alleyway and breaks down sobbing. The Party disengages from their snooping to discuss connections between the Right Hand and Scatterhawk; [[Rook]] and [[Ember]] bring up a smuggling tunnel connecting a Softer Touch to the Scatterhawk estate. [[Forge]] catches up to [[Irelyn]] and hugs her; she leans into it. She apologizes for giving his name and shard, saying she "could've said no like my father". [[Forge]] says it's fine, and that he want to help. She reveals her dad was smuggled out of Myth Drannor by the Red Hand, who sought to be paid for their services by the shard-armor scheme; he declined, so they turned to [[Irelyn]] to get the job done. She reveals the shards aren't for scrying; rather, it's to ease the process of teleport-aided assassinations, culminating with [[Queen Raedra Obarskyr]]. [[Irelyn]] says the Queen needs to be alerted. [[Forge]] says he'll get her family back, and to ~~slime up~~ lay low. They depart and The Party reconvenes.
