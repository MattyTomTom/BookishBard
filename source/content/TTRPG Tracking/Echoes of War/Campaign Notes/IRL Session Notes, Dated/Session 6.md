Sans [[Ysgraviir]], the party awakes in the [[Red Herring Inn & Tavern]], having had a much needed rest. Certain members decide it's high time to LOOT THE BODY!

- [[Ember]], [[Rook]], and [[Tharrick]] elect to loot the body of last night's vanquished foe. Unmasking the assailant, it's revealed the fellow had his lips ripped off and sewn shut, offering a macabre visage. [[Tharrick]] gets handsy with the corpse, laying his fingers on the deceased's bracelet, which lets off a blinding flash, transporting the Dragonborn to. . . the void? Carefully wandering around the black space, [[Tharrick]] reaches the conclusion he is in a windowless, entry/exitless containment cell. Eventually, a disembodies voice reaches out: "That is not a toy for you to use" is its statement, to which [[Tharrick]] apologizes for his foolishness. The voice informs [[Tharrick]] that it knows his name, and advises that he "cease his search", and demands payment for his safe travel back from the void; specifically, that [[Tharrick]] continue to wear the bracelet. After agreeing, he is transported back, no time having passed since his transportation. The trio head to the restaurant of the [[Red Herring Inn & Tavern]] to gather with the Party to eat.

- Having joined the Party for breakfast, [[Rook]] and [[Ember]] inform [[Ascánthir]] of [[Tharrick]]'s ill-ish gotten silver bangle gains. [[Tharrick]] gets defensive, pointing out the married couple's equally dangerous interest in poking at the Red Right Hand. [[Ascánthir]] requests to Identify the bangle, and is permitted to. The Identify attempt reveals the piece of jewelry is an unremarkable item that simply once held a "charge" for a spell, and is now inert. [[Ascánthir]] gives the bracelet back.

- [[Brannon]], having just finished eating, suddenly snaps his gaze towards the ceiling, and seemingly begins tracking some invisible flying entity. [[Ascánthir]] asks what the commotion is about, to which [[Brannon]] demands more food as payment for such information. [[Ascánthir]] disregards the request, using magic to see through [[Brannon]]'s eyes, revealing an invisible floating eye staring down at [[Rook]], [[Ember]], and [[Tharrick]]. [[Ascánthir]] informs the trio of the ocular phantom, and it disappears. [[Ascánthir]] recommends they look into getting magical amulets that would disallow such spying. He tells [[Ember]] that [[Crown Princess Adenia Obarskyr]] would be just the lady to ask for such an item, but advises not to be too nosy as to why she has so many magical accouterments. 

- [[Rook]], [[Ember]], and [[Tharrick]] head to the entrance of Castle Obarskyr. Their purpose of arrival is inquired upon by a Purple Dragon guard. After introductions are made, [[Rook]] requests to see [[Crown Princess Adenia Obarskyr]], and the trio is led to the Castle Gardens. It smells incredible, and is currently occupied by Adenia and [[Prince Baerovus Obarskyr]]. After a short talk with the trio, Adenia agrees to lead them to her collection. Baerovus is whispering along the walk, which [[Rook]] overhears: "That's the third attack this week, I'll alert [[Queen Raedra Obarskyr]]". [[Prince Baerovus Obarskyr]] departs, and [[Crown Princess Adenia Obarskyr]] arrives with the trio to a Castle tower. After advising a guard to leave them uninterrupted, she leads them into a physically impossible large room in the tower, filled with all kinds of fancy royal crap: tomes, fine inks, relics, and a rotating display case of various presumably magical items. She reveals an extensive collection of Amulets of Protection Against Magic and Detection, and offers for the trio to take their pick. [[Rook]] gets one shaped like a dirk; [[Ember]], a star; [[Tharrick]], an arrowhead. After they obtain their enchanted jewelry, [[Crown Princess Adenia Obarskyr]] makes a request of [[Tharrick]]: she has way too many suitors, but one in particular is a specifically annoying fellow, and needs to be publicly embarrassed to dissuade him from continuing to pursue her. She says she will point out this Lord Lordill at an upcoming ball for [[Tharrick]] to humiliate.

- [[Ven]], [[Ascánthir]], and [[Forge]] convene over breakfast. As they eat, [[Ven]] spots [[Zabrina Carpenterbee]] eating at a table with her troupe. Huh. [[Ascánthir]], noting the scalebound tome [[Ven]] acquired from Gil, requests to Identify it: it is revealed to be enchanted with virtual indestructibility, just like [[Ascánthir]]'s spellbook. This inspires [[Forge]] to test that claim, which is backed up by his failure to rip a page out. [[Ven]] shares a poem with them that he christened the new book with, marking a new beginning of sorts; he expresses how thankful he is to have found the Party: "These are the new good old days". [[Ascánthir]] responds in kind, and reminisces a bit about his time at Suzail University. [[Forge]] joins in the walk down memory lane, acknowledging his late mentor Silas, and laments his own lack of a greater personal goal to accomplish in [[Cormyr]]. Hearts having been worn on sleeves, [[Ven]] updates the fellows on his progress towards identifying a formal name for the Party. Acronyms are floated, and the pressure to name a group is acknowledged; [[Ven]] has never actually named an adventuring party before, despite having penned the tales of many. He suggests, a bit cheekily, operating under the name The Left Hand. The name provokes laughter, but the danger of such a moniker dampens its likelihood of sticking.

Silas was married, and that wife, Jayih, is still alive and well; [[Forge]] wrote her a letter to keep in touch. [[Ven]] opens up about Silphane, revealing that his own brother, Strife, killed her when [[Ven]] and Silphane, with an adventuring party, where ambushed by brigands, being led by Strife. The vile brother decided to merely beat [[Ven]] to near-death, promising to see him again someday. [[Ven]] reveals that he doesn't remember his parents, and warns that his other 3 siblings may be just as deranged. [[Ven]] then asks [[Ascánthir]] if he has any losses to speak of. [[Ascánthir]] speaks of how he came into possession of the family sword: his father died far earlier than expected or intended, but indicates some of the finer details are a bit confidential. [[Ascánthir]] follows up with some strange questions: what would you tell your lost loved one if you could tell them anything? [[Ven]]: Thank you. [[Forge]]: Everything will be okay. Super casually, [[Ascánthir]] then asks if the deceased's souls are trapped; after a short panic attack on [[Ven]]'s part, it's ascertained that the answer is no for both. [[Ascánthir]] offers to deliver those messages; [[Forge]] says yes, but [[Ven]] declines.

- Post-breakfast, [[Vaelithra]] is wandering around the Festival happenings, seeking answers to her religious journey. Seeking directions from a Purple Dragon Knight, she's pointed toward the Wisdom Weavers, where she might find a Silver Dragonborn. Making her way there, [[Vaelithra]] is offered flowers and a holy relic (a doll) by some merchants. Evading them, she makes her way in to Wisdom Weavers. A quiet establishment, she sees some nameplates above two doors: [[Marlymis]] and [[Otaar]]. Having wrapped things up with her client (this is essentially a therapist office), [[Marlymis]], a Silver Dragonborn, comes into the lobby, shakes her head upon meeting [[Vaelithra]]'s eyes, and hangs around for a bit. [[Otaar]] wraps up with his client and emerges from his office, chatting with [[Marlymis]] a bit. [[Vaelithra]], eavesdropping, finds that [[Marlymis]] doesn't wish to speak with her because she looks too sad :( [[Otaar]] says "Gus's body is ready to be picked up" and makes for the door, a key in hand. Intercepting the Dragonborn, [[Vaelithra]] says howdy, and introductions are exchanged. She makes clear her desire to speak with [[Marlymis]], and [[Otaar]] offers his services instead, making mention of his allegiance to Bahamut. [[Vaelithra]] mentions her coming tests, and [[Otaar]] requests to touch the canary feathers she wears; upon doing so, he says he can help, urging [[Vaelithra]] to accompany him. They eventually arrive at Gus's grave site, where [[Otaar]] speaks with the wife of the deceased, offering condolences. Discussion is had of a particular verse from a Holy Tome of Bahamut Worship: "A caged bird cannot see all sights, it must travel to know of the world". After assuring the mourner that Gus was proud to be their husband, [[Otaar]] produces a rock, seemingly sucking the corpse into it. Understandably, [[Vaelithra]] freaks a bit at this. [[Otaar]] responds by quizzing her on the meaning of the holy verse mentioned. [[Vaelithra]] philosophizes a bit, impressing him. Heading back to Wisdom Weavers, [[Otaar]] leads the way to his office, where he points out a trapdoor that, logically, should lead to the pants shop downstairs. Instead, it leads down via staircase to a massive room leading to a catacomb, brimming with what seem to be more rocks like the one [[Otaar]] used to store Gus. Upon depositing the stone on a shelf, it forms into the likeness of the deceased. [[Vaelithra]] asks who Gus was to [[Otaar]], and he reveals that he knew him for most of the 90 years that he lived. [[Otaar]] then requests [[Vaelithra]] reveal a secret she hasn't shared with the Party. [[Vaelithra]] speaks of time she spent with her mentor, and sins committed that she hopes to someday atone for. Upon being asked why she hasn't old of this story, she indicated she worries how the members of the Party would react to her not-so-kosher past actions. [[Otaar]] is pleased with how candid she's been, and speaks of the power of truth and love. Through honeyed words, he advises her to be truthful. [[Otaar]] then refers to the catacombs as his GOT. DANG. HOARD. [[Vaelithra]] then recommends that [[Otaar]], real name Otaaryliakkarnos, get out and date. The Dragon, whose name means He Who Walks The Path Of Love, agrees, and they part ways.

- [[Forge]] makes his way to the arena, and meets with [[Ember]], [[Rook]], and [[Tharrick]]. He joins them in competition, paying the 100 gold revive fee. They're told they'll face "The Beast", but are shortly informed they'll need to fight a cockatrice that bested the group before them first. It's . . . just a rooster? [[Tharrick]] opens the brawl with a loosed arrow, striking its wing, prompting the feathery foe to book it towards the four heroes. [[Rook]] responds by firing an arrow of his own, which finds purchase on the pissed-off poultry, which continues its pursuit. [[Ember]] yeets out a fiery magical blast, making rotisserie out of the cockatrice, tromping an uproarious applause from the crowd. The four are offered unnecessary medical treatment during a ten minute break, after which a countdown for The Beast's arrival begins. A monster erupts from the ground in the center of the arena, and land sharks towards the group.

- [[Ascánthir]] and [[Ven]] hit up The Golden Knowledge, where Gil meets them happily. Gil leads the pair THROUGH a bookcase, down some stairs, hands them (and [[Brannon]]) hats that magically disguise them, and they enter a black room with a dark sphere in its center. [[Ascánthir]] informs [[Ven]] he intends to cast Sending to Silas to deliver [[Forge]]'s message, and then another to his own father, who is trapped in a phylactery belonging to a Lich. [[Ven]] lends some poetic inspiration, and leaves [[Ascánthir]] to his work, electing to hang out with Gil during the proceedings. [[Ascánthir]]'s message to Silas is received, and the mentor replies, indicating that he knew [[Forge]] would do well, and to get them gosh danged Right Hand sons of guns. To his father[[Ascánthir]] sends "Father, I miss you, and I'm sorry I wasn't there. I hope this theory actually works. Can you help me in any way possible?" So much time passes that [[Ascánthir]] believes the attempt to be a bust. As he turns to leave the Black Room, a reply comes: "[[Ascánthir]], your mind ever impresses, but this connection is dangerous. I shall endeavor to help you, but He knows all. I miss you deeply." [[Ascánthir]] gets the impression that "He", the Lich, will get wind of this conversation eventually, and Sends again: "Well, if Morgoloth is going to know of this sometime, might as well help me in any way you can right now. HOW UPGRADE SWORD??" The response: "The sword is an extension of you, so you must learn more of yourself to learn it. . . . .I. . . . HeaR. . . . .yoU. . . . . YOUR SOUL WILL BE MINE"

- [[Ven]], in the main library area of The Golden Knowledge, reads about high social etiquette, seeking to avoid further blunders against [[Prince Erzoured Obarskyr]]. He learns that he didn't actually break any real law in his outings with [[Lady Sarya Crownsilver]], just stomped on some social norms. There is no explicit law banning non-nobles from marrying nobility. [[Ven]] then seeks to read up on [[Ysgraviir]]'s clan, and learns of their origins through a book penned by none other than [[Mistress Ossani Eveningspire]]. It tells of [[Ysgraviir]]'s clan hailing from the Stormhorns, fabled to be descendants of Giants. Due to their reputation of raiding and pillaging, [[Cormyr]] as a whole isn't their biggest fan; however, the clan has been more or less pardoned and treated as a heroic tribe due to wartime aid. Satisfied with his time in nonfiction, [[Ven]] asks Gil where he can find magical means to keep more books on his person; Gil recommends seeing [[Crown Princess Adenia Obarskyr]], which [[Ven]] bristles at, noting his current bad streak of luck with Royalty. Gil encourages him to seek to make better impressions. [[Ven]] then asks how Gil came into ownership of the library; the Dragon tells of his hatred from Chromatic Dragons, and his part in fighting the Great Black Dragon of yore; Gil was offered refuge by an Obarskyr ancestor, which Gil took advantage of whilst building his literary hoard. Gaining fortune from the spoils of the Chromatic Dragons he hunted, Gil eventually bought The Golden Knowledge, seeking to settle down. He notes that the current Royal Family knows of his identity; [[Queen Raedra Obarskyr]] steers clear of the place, while the others visit occasionally. [[Ascánthir]] returns from the Black Room, looking haggard; he shares of his success and misfortune, which inspires Gil to recommend more protective amulets. [[Ascánthir]] dismisses [[Brannon]], much to his chagrin.

- Following her adventure with [[Otaar]], [[Vaelithra]] has a conversation with [[Verdix]], who expresses his fear towards the revelation that [[Otaar]]'s hoard is a bunch of corpses. [[Verdix]] establishes he wants a hoard of fish. [[Vaelithra]] and her scaly companion make their way to the arena, where they meet [[Ven]] and [[Ascánthir]]. They all take their seats as spectators just in time for the main event, and they see [[Ember]], [[Rook]], [[Tharrick]], and [[Forge]] laying into The Beast, holding their own. [[Rook]] lands a blow on the foe's leg, causing it to stumble and allowing [[Forge]] to more easily dodge its strikes. [[Tharrick]] lobs a Strength-affecting curse, which finds purchase on the monster. An Agonizing Scorcher launched by [[Ember]] primes The Beast for a fresh layer of hurt, courtesy of [[Forge]]'s hammer. This enables an easy death blow to be landed on the grounded creature by [[Rook]], who runs his blade through its eye socket. The crowd absolutely loses it, and the victors head to the Royal Pavilion to receive their due reward. [[Queen Raedra Obarskyr]], along with the Royal Family and some nobles, expresses her pride and pleasure at the performance, and expresses willingness to impart gifts up to a certain prize value to the contestants. [[Forge]] requests a Cloak Of Protection while the others opt for a rain check. Departing the pavilion, the combatants meet up with [[Ven]], [[Ascánthir]], and [[Vaelithra]]. [[Queen Raedra Obarskyr]], also departing, gives short warm greetings. [[Ven]] delivers cold stares to both [[Lady Sarya Crownsilver]] and [[Prince Erzoured Obarskyr]]. [[Ascánthir]] runs down [[Crown Princess Adenia Obarskyr]] to request an amulet for [[Brannon]], and is scolded for being loose lipped regarding her collection. [[Ascánthir]] claims innocence, since he never said why she has such an impressive magical armament. The Party splits off again.
